COLOGNE CONSULTANT v3.1 — QOL/UI PROTOTYPE

This is a static web app. No build step, framework, database, or backend is required.

For Netlify Drop / manual deployment:
1. Deploy this folder as-is.
2. index.html must remain at the site root.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
4. manifest.webmanifest and service-worker.js must remain at the site root.

After deployment, open the HTTPS site in Safari on iPhone and use Share > Add to Home Screen.
